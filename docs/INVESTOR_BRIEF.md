# Investor Brief

## The thesis

Neurological classification changes while longitudinal evidence remains scattered across notes, tests, medication history, and patient reports. VLEP’s core idea is to preserve the evidence once, then resolve and compare a living phenotype under explicit classification versions.

## What is defensible today

- A working, inspectable software contract for evidence → assertions → CSEP.
- Tamper-evident provenance and deterministic replay.
- A version-aware mapping workflow that exposes uncertainty and review requirements.
- A credible validation and governance path that does not confuse a product demo with clinical evidence.

## Potential moat

The potential differentiator is not a generic prediction dashboard. It is the combination of an evidence ledger, a longitudinal multi-dimensional phenotype, and governed nosology reinterpretation with reproducible outputs.

That moat remains a hypothesis until the mapping registry, phenotype evidence base, expert review network, validation datasets, and institutional integrations exist.

## Near-term use of capital or partnership

1. Convert formal research documents into an executable, frozen probabilistic model with simulation-based verification.
2. Build the governed mapping registry and reviewer workflow.
3. Secure expert and institutional research partners.
4. Produce a pre-specified retrospective validation protocol and data-access plan.
5. Harden security, privacy, quality management, and regulatory strategy.

## Diligence questions this repository answers

- Can the core concept be demonstrated without real patient data? Yes.
- Is every transformation inspectable? Yes, within the MVP boundary.
- Are source claims separated from implemented evidence? Yes.
- Is there a credible path to clinical research? Yes, documented as future work.
- Is the product clinically validated today? No.
