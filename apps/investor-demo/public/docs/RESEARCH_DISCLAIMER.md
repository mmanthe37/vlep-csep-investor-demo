# Research Disclaimer

VLEP Research MVP is experimental research software demonstrated with fictional synthetic data.

It is not a medical device, is not clinically validated, and is not intended to diagnose, monitor, predict, prevent, or treat any disease or condition. It must not be used to make or support patient-care decisions.

All profile labels, scores, mappings, and timeline events in the public demo are generated from an authored synthetic fixture. “Demo score” values are deterministic software outputs, not calibrated clinical probabilities.

Terminology mappings can be incomplete or conditional. The application preserves original evidence and flags review requirements, but that behavior does not prove the clinical correctness of a mapped label.

Before any clinical research or deployment, the project requires institutional governance, privacy and security review, expert adjudication, bias analysis, retrospective and prospective validation, human-factors testing, and an applicable regulatory determination.

ILAE, standards organizations, healthcare providers, and academic institutions referenced in the documentation do not endorse this project.
